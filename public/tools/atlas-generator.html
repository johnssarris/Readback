Readback — Glyph Atlas Generator

Renders the 95 printable ASCII characters (0x20–0x7E) in JetBrains Mono into a sprite sheet + manifest for the glyph-matching pipeline. Uses JetBrains Mono Regular from Google Fonts by default; use "Load local font file" to render from your own .ttf/.otf instead if you want a pixel-exact match to what's installed on your machine. This is a dev-time tool — its output (atlas.png + atlas-manifest.json) gets placed under public/atlas/ in the project.

Using JetBrains Mono Regular (Google Fonts)
